About Me

I'm the founder of Ignis Solaris Studio—an indie studio grounded in psychology. Through narrative games, we offer gentle “micro-healing” for players facing universal loneliness and efficiency anxiety—small sanctuaries where you can feel unconditionally loved and cared for.
